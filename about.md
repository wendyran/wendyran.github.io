My name is Wendy (Ran) Wei. I'm an ML Engineer specializing in search, recommendation, and personalization systems — currently building ranking, relevance, and GenAI-powered search at Airbnb.

I grew up in China and got my bachelor's degree in Mathematics & Statistics from [Zhejiang University](http://www.zju.edu.cn/english), with an exchange year studying statistics at [Uppsala University](https://www.uu.se/) in Sweden. From there I went to [The Ohio State University](http://www.stat.osu.edu) for my PhD in Statistics (minor in Computer Science), working with [Dr. Elizabeth Stasny](https://stat.osu.edu/people/stasny.1) and [Dr. David Sivakoff](https://www.asc.ohio-state.edu/sivakoff.2/) on [network sampling and estimation theory](http://rave.ohiolink.edu/etdc/view?acc_num=osu1471846863). During grad school I interned as a Data Scientist at Twitter, and was part of the team that won the [2013 Capital One National Data Analytics/Modeling Competition](https://artsandsciences.osu.edu/news/statistics-graduate-students-win-national-data-analytics/modeling-competition).

That Twitter internship turned into a full-time role, and I've spent the decade since building large-scale ML systems across social, e-commerce, and marketplace platforms: personalizing the Explore Tab at **Twitter/X**, working on ads quality at **Pinterest**, building video recommendation systems at **Facebook/Meta**, founding **TigerLab AI** (an open-source toolkit for LLM safety evaluation) and teaching Python along the way, leading search retrieval and relevance work — including early GenAI/LLM initiatives — at **Coupang**, and now working on ranking, relevance, and personalization at **Airbnb**.

Alongside the day job, I write. I'm the author of *[Advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation: Bridging Large Language Models and Knowledge Graphs](https://www.amazon.com/Advanced-Retrieval-Augmented-Generation-Bridging-Knowledge/dp/1394374682)* (Wiley-IEEE Press), which bridges LLMs and knowledge graphs to help build AI systems that hallucinate less and reason better, and *[Data Science: Methods and Practice](http://cs.cmpedu.com/books/book/5610009.htm)* (数据科学：方法与实践, China Machine Press), a textbook spanning statistics, machine learning, and their applications in search, recommendation, and NLP. I also hold a couple of patents on detecting anomalous behavior on social platforms, and I keep a running [Conference Tracker](/conference-tracker/) and occasional [Tech Notes](/blogs/) from talks and workshops I find interesting.

Outside of work, I ski, surf, scuba dive, and occasionally jump out of planes; I play piano and guzheng, dance, and practice calligraphy; and I share my home with a couple of cats, a garden, and an ever-growing stack of books. If any of that overlaps with what you're working on, I'd love to hear from you — find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/wendyranwei), [GitHub](https://github.com/wendyran), or [Twitter/X](https://twitter.com/wendyranwei).
