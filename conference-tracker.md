My tracker for major AI / ML / LLM / NLP / IR conference submissions, grouped by general prestige tier. Verified against each conference's official site as of **July 12, 2026** — dates are cited below with sources. Entries marked "not yet officially posted" show the most recent confirmed prior-year cycle for reference only; do not treat those as real future dates. All deadlines are 11:59 PM AoE ("Anywhere on Earth", UTC-12) unless noted otherwise. Every entry now also lists a **Notification** date — useful for planning where to resubmit if a paper gets rejected (see the Resubmission Planner below).
